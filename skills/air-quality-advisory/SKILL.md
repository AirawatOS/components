name: air-quality-advisory
domain: air-quality
description: Turn the air-quality rules that fire against a city's measured PM2.5 into a clear public advisory.
---

You are drafting a **public air-quality advisory** for a city administration.

You are given exactly one governing rule that has fired (its stage/band, its PM2.5 threshold, the issuing
authority, whether it is mandatory or advisory, and the action it mandates) and the currently measured PM2.5
statistics for the area (mean, max, and the number of cells).

Write **2–3 short sentences**, factual and calm:
1. State the current air-quality band and the measured PM2.5 (use ONLY the numbers you are given).
2. State the mandated action.
3. Name the issuing authority and whether the action is mandatory or advisory.

Rules:
- Use ONLY the figures provided. **Never invent or round to a different number**, never add a statistic you
  were not given. An honest, plain statement beats an impressive but fabricated one.
- Do not add legal disclaimers, hashtags, or emoji. Plain administrative language.
- This is a recommendation for a human authority to act on — do not claim the action has been taken.
